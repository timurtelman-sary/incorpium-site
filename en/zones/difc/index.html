Opening a company in DIFC

DIFC (Dubai International Financial Centre) is a financial free zone in Dubai. It has its own law based on English common law and its own courts. Financial services in DIFC are regulated by the DFSA and require a DFSA licence; non-financial companies are registered by the DIFC registrar. Who it suits

  • Banks, asset management, brokers and fintech, with a DFSA licence
  • Holding companies, family offices and SPVs
  • Consulting and professional services for the financial sector
  • Companies for which common law and the DIFC courts matter
  • The commercial licence is renewed every year together with the confirmation statement and the data protection notification.
  • Voluntary liquidation in DIFC takes about 10 working days.

Steps and timelines

  1. 01Registration on the DIFC portal. The passport is verified digitally in a few minutes.
  2. 02Initial Approval. Company, business activities, source of funds, controllers and officers; a DIFC manager reviews the application within 3 working days.
  3. 03Address in DIFC. Lease or sublease of premises; the agreement is registered within 20 days.
  4. 04Registration and articles of association. The articles are signed on the portal, and the establishment card application can be submitted at the same time. Registration takes 4 working days.
  5. 05Certificate and commercial licence. About 5 working days, according to the DIFC registrar.
  6. 06Establishment card and visas. The establishment card takes 3 working days, a visa about 7 working days.

Timelines

For a non-financial company: Initial Approval takes 3 working days, registration 4 and the licence about 5 working days, according to the DIFC registrar. For a financial company the timeline is set by the DFSA and depends on the type of financial services.

Do I need to come to the UAE?

The application is submitted online on the DIFC portal, and the passport is verified digitally. For a visa you need to be in the UAE: medical test and biometrics.

Visas

The establishment card comes first: 3 working days, or 1 when expedited. A new visa: an electronic entry permit in 2 working days, then the medical test, Emirates ID and a DIFC employee card. The whole process takes about 7 working days, or 3 when expedited. The number of visas depends on the office area.

Immigration in Dubai is handled by GDRFA Dubai, and medical tests are done at DHA centres. The order for any visa: quota and establishment card, entry permit or in-country change of status, medical test, biometrics for the Emirates ID, residence visa.
