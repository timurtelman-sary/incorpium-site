Privacy policy

Version of 13 November 2026.

Who processes the data

The incorpium.com website and the Incorpium AI consultant belong to INCORPIUM - FZCO, DIEZA licence 39747, Dubai Silicon Oasis, DDP, Building A1, Dubai, UAE. Questions about data: info@incorpium.com.

What data we receive

From the request form: name, phone or WhatsApp number and the message text.

From the chat with the AI consultant: the messages you write, and your phone number if you leave it.

Technical data: IP address and browser information in server logs.

Why

To answer your question, calculate the cost and contact you about your request.

To improve the knowledge base and the consultant's answers. For this we use questions without names or phone numbers.

Who we share it with

The data is seen by the Incorpium staff who handle your request. We do not sell data or share it for advertising.

The consultant's answers are generated by a language model from an external provider; the dialogue text is passed to it. The hosting and database servers are run by external providers.

Cookies and browser storage

The site does not set advertising cookies. The chat may store a dialogue identifier in your browser to continue the conversation. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.

How long we keep data

Requests and correspondence are kept for as long as needed to work with you and as required by UAE law. You can ask us to delete your data: write to info@incorpium.com.

Your rights

You can find out what data we hold about you, correct it or ask for it to be deleted. We process data under UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on the Protection of Personal Data.
