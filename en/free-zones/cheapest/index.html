How we calculate

  • Zone and government fees from the Incorpium database: every figure has a source and a check date.
  • The first year and the renewal are calculated separately, for a package with no visa, one and two visas.

Questions about free zone costs

Which is the cheapest free zone in the UAE?

According to the Incorpium database as of 20 November 2026, the cheapest first year with one visa is in Creative City (from 10,569 AED), Ajman NuVentures (from 11,120 AED) and SRTIP (from 11,560 AED). With no visa, from 3,999 AED in Creative City and SRTIP. These are zone and government fees, excluding Incorpium services. Before choosing, compare the renewal and check that your business activity is allowed.

How much does a free zone company with two visas cost?

With two visas the cheapest first year is in SRTIP (from 15,835 AED), Ajman NuVentures (from 16,840 AED) and ADAFZ (from 16,908 AED). Each additional visa adds the quota, entry and processing, the medical test, Emirates ID and medical insurance.

Why is the cheapest package not always the best deal?

In some zones the first year is cheap, but the renewal costs more or is not published. A package may include only one business activity or limit the number of visas, and some business activities need a regulator's approval before the licence. Incorpium's consultant checks this for your case.

Which is cheaper: a free zone or the mainland?

With one visa the first year in free zones starts from about 10,569 AED (Creative City), on the mainland from 10,374 AED (Sharjah mainland) without the fees that depend on the business activity and the office. That is why the mainland total is best calculated for your case in the chat.

Does the ranking include Incorpium services?

No. The ranking includes only zone and government fees and minimum medical insurance. The consultant gives the full amount with Incorpium services in the chat, the first year and the renewal separately.
